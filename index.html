The Universal Need

The World Needs This Solution.
We Chose Africa First.

The surveillance ad-tech graph is collapsing — cookies deprecated, iOS privacy walls in place, regulators sharpening. The United States in particular faces a behavioral-data crisis: legacy intelligence stacks like Nielsen, Experian, and Acxiom are decaying, creators get scraps from algorithmic platforms, and members have lost any sense of agency over the data they generate. The world needs a participatory replacement. We're building it everywhere — starting where the proof is fastest.

Why the U.S. needs this — #1
The legacy intelligence stack is decaying
Nielsen panels miss connected viewing. Experian and Acxiom run on graph data that cookie deprecation, iOS ATT, and state privacy laws are dismantling in real time. U.S. institutional buyers are paying more for less reliable signal every quarter.
Why the U.S. needs this — #2
The creator economy is extracting from creators, not for them
U.S. creators generate billions of hours of attention and get back fractional ad-share crumbs. The structural fix isn't a better creator-fund — it's a model where members spend the money they earn directly on creators they value.
Why the U.S. needs this — #3
Members have lost data agency
Surveillance fatigue is real. Trust in platforms is collapsing alongside the regulatory framework that enabled them. Participatory data — consented, compensated, member-controlled — is the only model that survives the next decade of U.S. and global regulation.
Why the U.S. needs this — #4
No one has built the replacement
Every major U.S. platform is patching the old surveillance model with privacy theater. No incumbent has rebuilt around participatory, first-party, creator-funded behavioral signal — because doing so requires giving up the surveillance economics they were built on.

Why Africa first — the proof is fastest here

The problem is global. The proof is fastest where the model fits the market most naturally and the alternatives are weakest.

01 Mobile-Native From Day One No desktop legacy to carry. No browser-era assumptions. The flywheel runs on the device the buyer is already holding.
02 Creator-Dense Culture Afrobeats, Nollywood, gospel, comedy — globally exportable creator engines that provide natural engagement fuel.
03 Data-Starved Institutions African institutions have nothing comparable to Nielsen / Experian / Acxiom — and will pay for the signal we generate.
04 Lower CAC, Faster Iteration Cheaper to acquire users, faster to learn, regulatory breathing room to move at the speed the architecture requires.
05 If It Works Here, It Works Anywhere Hardest distribution; cheapest proof. Every subsequent market — Asia, LATAM, eventually U.S. and Western buy-side — is structurally easier and more profitable.

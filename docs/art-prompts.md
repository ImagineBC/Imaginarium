# Imaginarium Website — Art Prompts

## Visual Direction Reference
The Imaginarium town uses a **medieval fantasy craft** isometric style. Think Age of Empires meets a fantasy village builder. Wooden and stone buildings with thatched/shingled roofs, timber-frame construction, warm earthy tones (browns, warm wood, stone grays, terracotta). Cobblestone and dirt roads. Water channels and streams. Lush green grass and dense trees. The aesthetic is hand-crafted, artisan, warm — like a miniature medieval-fantasy village diorama.

**Key visual traits from the game prototype (kuntacontent.itch.io):**
- Isometric camera angle (~30-degree elevation)
- Medieval/fantasy wooden and stone architecture
- Thatched roofs, wooden beams, stone foundations
- Earthy warm color palette (browns, greens, warm wood tones, stone grays)
- Paved stone and dirt roads with clear pathways
- Water channels/streams winding through the town
- Lush green environment — dense grass, varied trees, hedges
- Buildings have personality — greenhouses, water wheels, wooden signs, chimneys
- Small details: barrels, crates, market stalls, lanterns, benches
- Level/progression UI elements visible (game-like feel)
- Overall vibe: welcoming, organic, craft-built, living medieval-fantasy community

Color palette context: The website uses a dark theme (navy/charcoal backgrounds), so art will be viewed on dark cards or through dark gradient overlays.

Warm golden daylight, soft shadows, rich earthy saturated colors. The village fills the full 16:9 frame and suggests even more beyond the edges. Miniature diorama quality — like a beautifully crafted tabletop village model.

## Delivery Notes

- **Style consistency is critical** — all village art must match the game prototype from kuntacontent.itch.io. Same medieval-fantasy craft style, same earthy warmth, same architectural language.
- Buildings: timber-frame, stone foundations, thatched/shingled roofs, wooden signs, warm-lit windows
- Environment: lush green grass, varied trees, water channels, stone bridges, wildflowers
- Character figures should be simple, warm, recognizable but not overly detailed
- All art must read well on dark navy (#0a0e1a) backgrounds
- Deliver as PNG with transparency where the background is custom, or baked dark background
- **Retina resolution required** — deliver at 2x the noted pixel dimensions
- File naming: `hero-town.png`, `panel-members.png`, `panel-brands.png`, `panel-creators.png`, `panel-investors.png`, `map-africa.png`, `sponsorship-visual.png`
- Place delivered files in `Documents/website/images/`

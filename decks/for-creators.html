10% Floor
The Structural Guarantee

By design, at least 10% of every dollar brands inject into the platform must be spent on creator content. Not a marketing claim. Not a goal. A rule baked into the economy — brands literally cannot put money in without that share flowing to you and your peers.

Compared to the old way

Other platforms pay creators a fraction of ad revenue — pennies for thousands of views, with no floor and no guarantee. Here, every member who shows up is a paying member by design, and a structural minimum of brand spend is contractually owed to creator content.

Stream 02 — Referral Income

Your following is your annuity.

Bring your audience over. They earn from campaigns. They spend on creators (sometimes you, sometimes others — doesn't matter). And you earn passive income from their on-platform activity, for as long as they're members.

The loyalty you've already built — that thing every other platform takes for granted and pays you nothing for — becomes a long-term, recurring revenue stream. Not a signup bonus. Not a one-time bump. An annuity.

Compared to the old way

On every other platform, your community is their asset. Subscriber list, follower count, engagement graph — theirs. Here, your referred members pay you back forever. Your community is your asset, in cash form.

Stream 03 — Sponsor-Funded Reach

Brands pay for the audience to come to you.

Sponsors on the platform fund member access to creator content. When members spend that sponsor-funded access on your work, you get paid. Every time. The brand foots the bill so the audience reaches you for free.

It's a third revenue stream that does not exist anywhere else. Patreon doesn't have it. YouTube doesn't have it. TikTok, Instagram, Substack, Cameo — none of them. It's structurally unique to a platform where brands, members, and creators all share the same flywheel.

Compared to the old way

Where else does the brand pay for the audience to come to you? Nowhere. Sponsorship on every other platform means *you* serve the brand's content to *your* audience. Here, the brand serves *your* content to *the* audience. The flow flips.
